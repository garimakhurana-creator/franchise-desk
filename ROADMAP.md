# ROADMAP

What would make the desk better with data the case did not provide.

1. **True catchments instead of pin centres.** Pin boundary polygons and drive-time isochrones would replace the 2 km / 5 km rings with real overlap areas, and spacing between new stores could use travel time rather than straight-line distance.
2. **Store telemetry for cannibalisation and overflow.** Sales, footfall and order-fulfilment data from existing stores would show which stores are saturated (good places to add a nearby store) and which are fragile (protect them). The adapter and a watermarked simulation toggle are the intended hook; nothing simulated touches a real decision.
3. **Real rent and fit-out costs.** Replace the seeded rent index with broker or registry data per pin, and fit-out quotes per format, so the funds check reflects the street, not the tier.
4. **Verified identity and documents.** KYC, PAN and bank-statement verification APIs would turn "claimed" facts into "verified" automatically and close integrity checks faster.
5. **Surroundings and demand.** Places and footfall providers (relative busyness, anchor stores) as a small, capped, switchable score input, labelled "estimated, relative".
6. **Partner portal.** Applicants upload documents and see their own status and next step; the same engine output drives both views.
7. **Learning from outcomes.** Compare predicted payback and opening dates with actuals to tune the illustrative weights, with every change versioned in the policy.
